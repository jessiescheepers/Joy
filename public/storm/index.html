the way we work
doesn't work

We hire people to do great work, then put their performance in their manager's hands. To see it, they must ask: how am I doing? To drive it, they must self-promote.

your performance,
in your hands

You should be the first one to know how you're doing. Joy clears the work-about-work (comms, planning, to-dos) that gets in the way of the work. And as you work, Joy builds your weekly retro: how your focus fed your goals, how your follow-through went, where you could grow - and helps you act on it as your week unfolds.

let the work
speak for itself

What you do with your progress is up to you: sharpen your focus, become more dependable, or share it with your lead ahead of a 1:1. No more brag sheets. No more rehearsing your impact. The work speaks for itself.

that feeling
of flying

How work is done separates the best teams from the rest. Joy is built for teams who chase that feeling of flying, of doing great work - together. Team retros keep you pointing in the same direction: the week's highlights, key contributions, where to grow next. Catch the small stuff before it adds up.

built for people,
by People

We're building Joy with Design Partners who believe in the power of their people. As ex-People people, we know this can only be built with humans in the middle of it all. We're looking for high-performing companies who want to grow their performance cultures.

Joy gets: one of your teams using Joy regularly - our first real users.
Design Partners get: free Joy, a product built around your people, and to be the first.
